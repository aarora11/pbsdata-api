About

PBS Lookup shows the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme schedule in plain language: what a medicine costs, what it takes to get it, and who can prescribe it.

What this site is not

It isn't medical advice, and it never recommends a medicine or a brand. It doesn't cover side effects, interactions, dosing, or prices outside the PBS. For any decision about your medicines, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

Where the data comes from

The Australian Government's PBS API, published by the Department of Health, Disability and Ageing. This site is updated when each monthly schedule is released. It is independent and not endorsed by the Australian Government. How we check this data.

Pharmacist view

The switch in the header shows everything PBS publishes for each item: item codes, dispensed prices, quantities in units and packs, dispensing rules, restriction and streamlined authority codes, notes and cautions. Add ?view=pharmacist to any link to open it that way.
