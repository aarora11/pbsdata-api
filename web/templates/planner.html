      How this estimate works

      • Prices are the PBS's own, from the {{ site.month_label }} schedule: the most a pharmacy can charge per script, and the amount PBS lets a pharmacy record towards your Safety Net.
      • Your real cost may be lower. Many pharmacies charge less than the PBS maximum, especially for lower-priced medicines. Ask your pharmacist how any discount affects your Safety Net.
      • The Safety Net counts your whole family's scripts together. Add everyone's medicines here to see your family's total. It resets every 1 January.
      • Brand premiums don't count towards the Safety Net, and you still pay them after reaching it.
      • Once you reach it, general patients pay the concession price for the rest of the year, and concession card holders pay nothing (apart from any brand premium).
      • Some repeat scripts filled too soon after the last one may not count. Your pharmacist can tell you, and keeps your Prescription Record Form.

      An estimate for planning, not a quote. Thresholds for {{ site.month_label[-4:] }}: {{ site.threshold_general | money }} general, {{ site.threshold_conc | money }} concession, as published by Services Australia.
